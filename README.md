## Animated background
Every page has an animated particle background: a field of small particles gathers into a glowing ring that follows your mouse, and drifts across the page on its own when the mouse is still. Hold the mouse button on an empty spot and let go to make the particles burst outward.

- **Light mode:** the Magnet preset (violet and pink)
- **Dark mode:** the Orbit preset (icy blues, with a slowly spinning ring)

It switches with the light/dark toggle, uses fewer particles and a dimmer layer on phones, and the site works normally without it if a browser doesn't support WebGL2.

The background is the [Antigravity](https://reactbits.dev/tools/background-studio?bg=antigravity) component from [React Bits](https://reactbits.dev) by David Haz, ported to plain JavaScript in `background.js`.
