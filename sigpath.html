SigPath

TypeScript Next.js Solana · Anchor (Rust) Live Hackathon

"Deal Go – Scam Towed." SigPath searches live marketplace listings at once, shows the best price with shipping included, and checks every listing for scam signs before you buy. Pay through SigPath and your money waits in an on-chain escrow until the order ships, or comes back to you automatically.

My role: a solo project, built for the Solana × Superteam Germany hackathon.

It's live: try the real thing live on Vercel + Solana devnet
Open SigPath → Try a search: AirPods Pro See the on-chain proof

Searches pull real listings from eBay Germany through the official eBay Browse API. Payments run on Solana devnet, the test network, so no real money is involved.

Every listing gets one verdict

PriceIs it plausible for its condition, compared with identical live listings?
PhotoIs the same picture used by other sellers?
AccountIs the seller's account brand new?
ReportsHas a verified buyer reported this seller for fakes?

The result is SigPath-checked, Look closer (with the reason), or Not price-checked when there is nothing to compare against. The page says plainly that "checked" means checked, not guaranteed.

Why it needs a blockchain

Escrow instead of trust. You pay in USDC into an escrow program on Solana. The seller is only paid once the order is fulfilled; if it isn't, anyone can trigger the refund, and the money can only go back to the buyer.

Reports only from real buyers. Only the wallet that paid for an order can report it, with a live photo of what arrived, and the seller gets 7 days to reply before a report can be upheld.

Penalties that follow the seller. An upheld finding is published to the Solana Attestation Service under the seller's marketplace handle, so anyone can check it without trusting SigPath's own database. Verified sellers earn a badge token that can't be transferred or sold, and an upheld report burns it.

Built with

Next.js 14, React and TypeScript on Vercel; two Anchor programs in Rust on Solana; the Solana Attestation Service; the eBay Browse API; Google Gemini for Ai-chan, the shopping assistant; plus a browser extension, price-drop alerts and a German / English interface.
